🚨 Raise Incident — IR Playbook

SLA (Industry Standard): CRITICAL = 1 hr acknowledge / 4 hr contain • HIGH = 4 hr / 24 hr • MEDIUM = 24 hr / 72 hr • LOW = 72 hr

Playbook: 1. Identify → 2. Contain → 3. Eradicate → 4. Recover → 5. Lessons Learned. Har incident me ye 5 steps tick karne hain.
